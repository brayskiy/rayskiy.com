01. About Me

I'm an Android and AI engineer. Right now I'm at Paramount, working on the Paramount+ app and building AI agents and hooks (Claude, OpenAI) to automate software development and testing. I contribute to open-source AI projects including AI Studio and Ollama.

Over 15+ years I've architected and delivered mobile apps for major brands — HBO, High 5 Games, A&E Networks, iROKOtv, Stash, and Walmart — spanning streaming, casino gaming, fintech, and e-commerce, and I've led and mentored engineering teams across the US, UK, Nigeria, and Poland.
