01. About Me

I'm a software engineer who has been publishing on the Google Play Store since 2012. My apps — from the GameWeave gaming platform to card games like Preferans and Omaha Hi/Lo Poker — have collectively earned millions of downloads and tens of thousands of ratings.

Beyond the app store, I enjoy building things from first principles: a minimalist LLM written from scratch in Kotlin, a scientific plotting library in C++, and my own YACC-based scripting language. I care about clean design, performance, and understanding systems all the way down.

Technologies I work with:
